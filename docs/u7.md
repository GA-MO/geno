# U7: glass, the composer rainbow and a green-and-white Noodle

The chat panel has a new default look, glass. `gcode.ui.style` switches between `glass` and `native`, the Trail styling from U6c, without a reload. High contrast themes always get native.

## What glass is

- Dark themes get dark glass: a plain near-black surface (`#0a0a0a`) with translucent white panes, soft edges and a backdrop blur. Light themes get light frosted glass: a plain near-white surface with translucent white panes and soft dark edges. Each kind is one set of CSS custom properties in `packages/extension/src/chat/webview.css`, and one set of rules reads them.
- Surfaces carry no hue. The user's bubble is neutral glass too. Only the rainbow edge, the status colors (green done, amber waiting, red failed), syntax colors and Noodle carry color.
- The composer floats over the transcript, so text that scrolls under it shows through the blur. The transcript scrolls focused or revealed elements to stop above the composer, never under it.
- Primary actions are neutral and solid: white with dark text on dark glass, near-black with white text on light glass, since a white button on light glass would have no contrast. Their focus ring sits outside the button. Secondary actions stay quiet glass.
- A rainbow edge runs around the composer all the time, and nowhere else. It holds still under reduced motion.
- The Trail rail is a 1px hairline, with dots flowing into the step under way or waiting on the user. Setting `--rail-finished` to `var(--rail-dots)` on `.rail` makes the whole rail dotted.
- Scrollbars are 6px, faint at rest and full while the pointer is over the scroller.

`gcode.ui.showChanges`, off by default, brings back the Changes view; Source Control lists the same files, and the changes card and the editor lenses review edits either way.

## Noodle

Box A is green with a white belly and one dark eye with its shine; the spots and blush are gone. `prototypes/u6/dinos/make_dinos.py` draws it, on a plain dark glass tile for the 128px icon.

## Cost of the rainbow

The rainbow turns a still conic gradient with a CSS transform, which the compositor animates without the panel's main thread. Main-thread busy time of the panel's renderer, from 3 s DevTools traces, median of 5 runs per side, alternated:

| Panel state | Rainbow running | Rainbow held |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Idle, after a turn | 9 ms (range 4 to 9), 60 fps | 3 ms (range 2 to 28), 0.3 fps |
| Streaming an answer | 236 ms (range 166 to 252), 60 fps | 243 ms (range 143 to 252), 60 fps |

The first version animated an `@property` angle instead; idle, it cost 142 ms (range 117 to 169) per 3 s, since every frame restyled and repainted the ring. While streaming, most of the cost is the panel's other animations, such as the shimmering "working" label and the caret. `GCODE_SCREENSHOTS=<dir> GCODE_PERF=1 pnpm vitest run packages/extension/test/style.integration.test.ts` reruns the traces and every capture below.
