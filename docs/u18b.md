# U18b: the working dino, and the peek while typing

Noodle now works alongside the turn. While a turn runs, a 48 px Noodle stands beside its current step, in the pose of the work under way. When the turn ends it plays one move, then becomes the still 24 px avatar of the finished turn. Over the composer, Noodle leans on the top edge, but only while the user types. The brief is `docs/u18-brief.md`: "In the chat", "The peek, upgraded" and the U18b line under "Split".

## The working dino

It is the sprite at 2×, a canvas at the right of the running turn's current step: the rail's last step, before the changes card or the changed files. While it shows, the turn's 24 px avatar steps aside, so there is one Noodle at a time. Finished turns are still.

| Work | Pose |
| --- | --- |
| waiting on the model, no call under way | thinking (dots) |
| `read`, `grep`, `glob`, `list`, `skill` | reading (notebook) |
| `edit` of a code file, by extension (`.ts`, `.py`, `.css`, …) | coding (laptop pull-out once a turn, then typing with glasses) |
| `edit` of anything else (`.md`, `.json`, configs, files with no extension) | writing (pencil) |
| `bash`, `check`, any `mcp__` call | running in place |
| any step of a Plan turn | planning (clipboard) |
| a card waiting on the user: approval, remember or `ask_user` | asking ("?") |
| the latest call failed or was denied, and none runs | sweat |

The order of precedence is asking, then sweat, then planning, then the call under way, then thinking.

- A call that starts and finishes between two renders, as most reads do, still shows its pose.
- A pose holds for one pass of its frames and at least 20 ticks (about 1.4 s) before thinking takes over. A new kind of work takes over at once.

**Turn end.** A turn that is done flexes when its last check passed. Checks are the `check` call and the check an edit runs after itself; the last verdict decides. A done turn without a passing check hops or somersaults, picked at random. A stopped or failed turn plays no move.

## The peek

The peek is one body at 2× (48 px, 46 px above the edge at full climb): the head, the neck, the chest with its white belly, and arms to two 3×2 hands with darker knuckles (`#4fae86`) that grip the composer's top edge over the rainbow. The box ends 1 px under the edge, so the composer clips the body, and climbing and ducking slide all of it.

- It climbs once the composer holds three typed characters, once per message. The first climb of the page ends with a wave.
- While the user types, its eyes look down toward the text, with a blink now and then.
- After 3 s without typing it plays one move, drum fingers or look around (ending with a look toward the pointer), then waits for the next pause.
- It ducks when the message is sent or the composer is emptied. It stays hidden while a turn runs, on the start page, and while the chat is idle.

## Motion budget

The dino and the peek each run on their own motion gate, as the start page does. They tick at 70 ms only while shown, the window has the focus and the view is visible. Under reduced motion or high contrast, the dino shows each pose's first loop frame and skips the turn-end move. The peek shows only the still head and hands, with no moves and no gaze.

**Cost.** The profile compares ten seconds of a running turn with Noodle thinking against the same turn with Noodle resting, three runs each. The numbers are in `docs/media/u18b/perf.json`.

- Noodle's timer callbacks, ticks and canvas paints together, took 34 to 41 ms per 10 s. The turn's other timers took about 3.5 ms. Noodle therefore costs about 0.3 to 0.4% of the panel's main thread, under the 2% target.
- The whole running turn keeps the main thread 6.6 to 8.7% busy either way, and draws 600 frames in 10 s whether Noodle moves or not. That cost comes from the turn's own CSS animations (the rainbow edge, the shimmer, the rail's flow and the spinner), not from Noodle.

## Where it lives

- `packages/extension/src/chat/sprite.ts`.
  - `POSES` gains `somersault`.
  - `PEEK_MOVES` holds the peek's moves as `Pose<PeekFrame>` rows: climb, typing, look around, drum, wave and duck.
  - `composePeek` draws a `PeekFrame` (eye, each hand's place, sink).
  - `playhead` picks any pose's frame at a tick.
- `working.ts` is pure.
  - `WORK_POSES` is the pose table, and `workOf` reads a turn's work.
  - `checksPassed` and `endingOf` decide the turn-end move.
  - `steer` advances the dino's `Working` state.
- `working-dino.ts` draws it into the rail.
- `peek-model.ts` is pure: `peekNext` moves a `Peek` between acts on `typed`, `sent`, `blocked`, `quiet` and `played`. `peek.ts` draws it.
- Unit tests: `packages/extension/test/working.test.ts`, `sprite.test.ts` and `start-page.test.ts`.
- Integration tests:
  - `working.integration.test.ts` covers the poses per step, flex, sweat, asking, planning, reduced motion and the CPU profile.
  - `start.integration.test.ts` covers the peek.
