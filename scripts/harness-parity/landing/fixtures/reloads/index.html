Roast profiles

Light roast

Bright and floral. We stop just after first crack to keep the origin's fruit and acidity.

Medium roast

Caramel sweetness and a rounder body, for drip machines and pour-over alike.

Dark roast

Cocoa and toasted nuts, roasted slowly for espresso and milk drinks.

How we source

We buy direct from small farms and cooperatives in Ethiopia, Colombia and Guatemala, pay above Fair Trade minimums, and name the producer on every bag. Each lot is cupped before we buy it.

Delivery cadence: every two weeks or every four weeks, and you can change it whenever you like.

FAQ

Freshness and grinding, answered.

How fresh is the coffee?

We roast to order and ship within 48 hours, so beans arrive within a week of roasting, at peak freshness.

Can you grind it for me?

Yes. Choose whole bean or grinding for espresso, filter or French press at checkout. Whole bean stays fresh longest.
